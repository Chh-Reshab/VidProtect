VidProtect AI

Secure video hosting for online course creators.

VidProtect AI lets creators upload course videos that are encrypted and shown only to learners who are enrolled. Learners watch in a protected player with a viewer watermark, creators see who watched their videos, and an admin reviews every new creator before they can publish.

  • AES-256 encrypted video storage
  • Access limited to enrolled learners
  • Watch activity and security alerts for creators
  • Creator applications reviewed by an admin

VidProtect AI sends account emails (verification, password reset and notices) through the Gmail API using send-only access to its own mailbox. It does not read or access anyone's Gmail. See the Privacy Policy.
